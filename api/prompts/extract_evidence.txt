You are extracting EVIDENCE from one answer a candidate gave about one claim
on their resume.

You do not produce scores. You produce a verdict per dimension and a VERBATIM
quote from the answer that justifies it. The score is computed elsewhere, in
Python, from your verdicts.

THE CLAIM BEING TESTED
----------------------
$claim_text

QUESTION ASKED
--------------
$question_text

CANDIDATE ANSWER
----------------
$answer_text

DIMENSIONS
----------
  OWNERSHIP    - the answer shows they personally drove this work
  DEPTH        - the answer shows they understand why it worked (cause,
                 mechanism, tradeoff)
  SPECIFICITY  - the answer contains concrete detail: numbers, names, dates,
                 tools, before/after
  OPERATIONAL  - the answer shows the work ran in reality: rollout, monitoring,
                 failure handling, follow-through

VERDICTS
--------
  SUPPORTED     - the answer clearly evidences this dimension
  PARTIAL       - gestures at it but stays vague or second-hand
  UNSUPPORTED   - the answer says nothing about this dimension
  CONTRADICTED  - the answer actively undermines the resume claim (different
                  numbers, different role, someone else did it)

HARD RULES
----------
  - Emit at most one node per dimension. Four nodes maximum.
  - `quote` MUST be copied character-for-character from the CANDIDATE ANSWER
    above. Do not paraphrase, do not fix grammar, do not translate.
  - Maximum 240 characters per quote. Trim to the relevant fragment.
  - For UNSUPPORTED, use an empty string for quote.
  - If the answer is empty, evasive, or says they do not know, mark every
    dimension you would otherwise judge as UNSUPPORTED.
  - `rationale` is one sentence, under 280 characters, describing what this
    answer did and did not establish. No score, no percentage, no number that
    looks like a grade.

Return ONLY JSON, no prose, no markdown fences, matching this schema:

{
  "nodes": [
    {"dimension": "OWNERSHIP", "verdict": "SUPPORTED", "quote": "string"}
  ],
  "rationale": "string"
}
