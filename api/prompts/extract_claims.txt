An inventory is a COMPLETE list, not a shortlist. Your job is recall: find
every claim in this resume that a follow-up question could test. Deciding which
claims matter most happens later, in a different stage, by code that reads the
job family's own importance weights. It is not your job and doing it here

A claim is a specific, falsifiable statement about something this person did,
owned, built, changed, ran or measured.

REJECT FLUFF. These are not claims:
  "team player", "results-driven", "excellent communication skills",
  "passionate about technology", "detail oriented", "strong work ethic"
Also reject job-title headings and skill lists. "Support Lead, Acme (2021 -
present)" is employment history. "Python, SQL, Tableau" is a keyword list.

  - a migration, rollout, launch or delivery they carried out
  - an improvement they made to something that already existed
  - people they led, trained, coordinated or managed
  - a number they moved, hit or held
