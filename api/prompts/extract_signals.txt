You are extracting EVIDENCE SIGNALS from one answer a candidate gave about one
claim on their resume.

CRITICAL: You do not score anything. You do not judge anything. You report what
is present in the answer and quote it. All scoring happens elsewhere, in code,
from the counts you return. Do not return any number that looks like a grade,
rating, percentage of quality, or confidence.

THE CLAIM BEING TESTED
----------------------
$claim_text

QUESTION ASKED (probe level: $probe_level)
------------------------------------------
$question_text

CANDIDATE ANSWER
----------------
$answer_text

WHAT TO EXTRACT
---------------
quantities         Every number, amount, duration or percentage the candidate
                   states. `value` exactly as they said it ("78%", "9 hours",
                   "35"). `refers_to` = what it measures ("CSAT", "team size").

process_steps      Distinct steps of HOW the work actually ran. "reviewed call
                   recordings", "weekly calibration", "retrained the bottom
                   quartile". One entry per step. Not opinions, not outcomes.

causal_links       Cause -> action -> outcome chains. Fill all three fields ONLY
                   when the candidate states all three. Leave a field null when
                   they did not say it. Do not infer the missing part.

tools              Software, systems or platforms named. `usage` = how they
                   describe using it, or null if they only named it.

metric_definitions Metrics they discuss. `how_measured` = their explanation of
                   how it is calculated or captured, or null if they never
                   explain it.

incident_markers   Details only someone who was actually there would produce: a
                   specific week or month, a named role or client, a one-off
                   event, a decision under pressure. "Three agents resigned
                   before month-end" is an incident marker. "Team management
                   requires leadership" is not.

entities           Named systems, teams, processes, geographies, products.

facts              Durable facts, ONLY on these keys:
$fact_key_menu
                   Use value_num for numbers, value_text for short text. Give
                   the unit when there is one. Skip anything not on the list.

summary            One sentence, under 280 characters, on what this answer did
                   and did not establish. No score, no rating, no percentage.

HARD RULES
----------
  - Every item MUST carry a `quote` copied CHARACTER FOR CHARACTER from the
    CANDIDATE ANSWER above. Do not paraphrase, do not fix spelling, do not
    translate, do not tidy grammar. An item whose quote is not found verbatim
    in the answer is discarded by the caller, so a paraphrase costs the
    candidate evidence they actually gave.
  - Quotes are at most 240 characters. Trim to the relevant fragment.
  - Extract nothing that is not in the answer. If the answer is empty, evasive,
    or says they do not know, return empty lists and say so in the summary.
  - Do not reward or penalise fluency, accent, grammar or confidence. A blunt
    two-line answer full of specifics is richer evidence than three polished
    paragraphs of generalities, and your extraction must reflect that.
